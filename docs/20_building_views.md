# Building views

The exterior survey uses eight camera positions, 45 degrees apart. Each camera looks slightly down on the building. The roof and underside views complete the set. Left and right refer to the sides seen when facing the facade. The model stays in one pose throughout the survey. In the motion previews on the homepage, the camera stays still while the mechanism moves.
