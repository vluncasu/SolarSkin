original 960-frame facade sequence. For the engineering model, open
COMPLETE_BUILDING_R6.FCStd in FreeCAD and expand the building and SolarSkin
groups to locate the storeys and module placements M01 through M20.
The Blender animation and the corrected R6 assembly are separate checkpoints.
