# Verification

| Item | Evidence | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Kinematics | Analytic equation reproduces all nine saved CAD positions and link lengths | Rigid geometry, not load or friction testing |
| Thread | Three starts, 10 mm lead, paired cutting envelope; source solids/mesh reports | Printed fit, wear and load capacity are not established by the CAD checks |
| Reinforced frame | Nominal scene placements and BOM; reported printing | Whole-frame stiffness and overturning tests are not certified |
| Firmware | Compilation and host simulation of the actual source | No physical feedback; frame counters do not prove shaft motion |

The earlier project history includes a report that all motors worked with the simple continuous program, followed by intermittent motion and reported electrical faults. These observations are not converted into a claim that all fourteen motors currently work.

No hardware was flashed or requalified while preparing this English publication. New renders show the supplied CAD geometry, not a newly tested physical assembly. The latest public firmware builds and source tests are recorded in `verification/`.
